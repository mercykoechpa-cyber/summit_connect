Summit Prep Ladder

High-Altitude Acclimatization Ladder

Conquering Point Lenana, Batian, or Kilimanjaro requires strategic altitude progression. Follow our 7-step East Africa acclimatization prep program from beginner hills to high alpine passes before your summit push!

Hike 1 (Warmup Base)

Kilimambogo (Ol Donyo Sabuk)

2,145m Altitude • 14 km Return • Gentle / Moderate

Gentle forest trail through Ol Donyo Sabuk National Park. Essential starter hike to break in your hiking boots, test backpack weight, and build baseline leg endurance.

Starter Trial
Hike 2 (Rift Escarpment)

Kijabe Hills Hike

2,680m Altitude • 13 km Escarpment Ridge • Moderate - Steep

Steep Great Rift Valley escarpment trek through dark railway tunnels, indigenous pine forests, and wind-blown rocky ridges to condition your cardiovascular engine.

Escarpment Cardio
Hike 3 (Aberdare Moorland)

Mount Kipipiri (Aberdares)

3,349m Altitude • 16 km Return • Challenging

Wild, dense Aberdare bamboo forest and tussock moorland climb. Superb high-altitude preparation for cold crosswinds, mud terrain, and sustained vertical gain.

Moorland Booster
Hike 4 (Alpine Gorges)

Mt. Kenya: Lake Michaelson Alpine High Hike

3,980m - 4,250m Altitude • 18 km Alpine Traverse • Strenuous

Dramatically scenic high-altitude trek down and up the Gorges Valley. Tests oxygen uptake above 3,900m while trekking past glacial waterfalls and sheer 300m rock walls.

High Alpine Test
Hike 5 (Volcanic Caldera)

Mount Elgon (Koitobos Peak / Wagagai)

4,222m - 4,321m Altitude • 3 - 4 Days Caldera Traverse • Endurance

Vast ancient volcanic caldera exploration. Multi-day pack-carrying trek across Kitum elephant caves, endemic giant groundsels, and thin-air ridges on the Kenya-Uganda border.

Caldera Endurance
Hike 6 (Vertical Day Push)

Teleki Valley Mackinder's Day Dash (Mount Kenya)

4,300m Altitude • 22 km Rapid Push • High Altitude Speed

The iconic fast acclimatization dash from Met Station (3,050m) through the Vertical Bog up to Mackinder's Camp (4,300m) and descent. Stimulates rapid red blood cell production.

4,300m Day Dash
Hike 7 (The 4,500m Benchmark)

Mount Meru (Socialist Peak, Tanzania)

4,562m Altitude • 4 Days Ridge Trek • Strenuous Peak

The premier training peak in East Africa. Climbing the narrow ash-cone crater rim at 4,562m provides unmatched high-altitude exposure, ensuring 99% summit readiness for Kilimanjaro & Batian.

4,500m Milestone
THE FINAL SUMMITS

Mount Kenya (Pt. Lenana 4,985m / Batian 5,199m) & Kilimanjaro (5,895m)

4,985m - 5,895m • 5 - 7 Days Summit Expedition

Thoroughly acclimatized, cardio-tested, and ready for 99.8% summit success alongside your Super Guide or community Hiking Group!
