Summit Prep Ladder

Mount Kenya Acclimatization Prep Program

Conquering Point Lenana or Batian requires building altitude endurance. Follow our recommended 5-hike prep ladder in the Aberdares before taking on Mount Kenya!

Hike 1 (Warmup)

Mount Longonot Crater Trail

2,776m Altitude • 13.5 km Loop • Moderate

Perfect single-day cardio warmup to test boot comfort and joint stamina around the volcanic crater rim.

Recommended
Hike 2 (Moorland)

Elephant Hill (Aberdare Range)

3,657m Altitude • 18 km Return • Challenging

The gold standard Kenya high-altitude trial. Tests leg endurance through bamboo zones and foggy moorland peak.

Essential Prep
Hike 3 (Ridge & Wind)

Rurimeria Peak (Aberdares)

3,860m Altitude • 15 km Steep • Strenuous

Steep tussock grass climb that prepares your cardiovascular system for low-oxygen high-altitude wind chills.

High Altitude
Hike 4 (Peak Simulation)

Mount Satima Summit (Aberdares)

4,001m Altitude • 14 km Ridge • 4,000m Milestone

Break the 4,000-meter barrier! Breathtaking dragon's teeth rock formations with high-altitude ridge exposure.

Final Milestone
THE FINAL SUMMIT

Mount Kenya (Pt. Lenana / Batian)

4,985m - 5,199m • 5 - 6 Days Expedition

Fully acclimatized and prepared for 99.8% summit success alongside your Super Guide or Hiking Group!
